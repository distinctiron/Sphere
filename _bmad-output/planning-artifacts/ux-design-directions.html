Attention Is All You Need

Ashish Vaswani Noam Shazeer Niki Parmar

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks that include an encoder and a decoder. The best performing models also connect the encoder and decoder through an attention mechanism.

We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer

A type of neural network architecture that relies entirely on self-attention mechanisms, discarding recurrence and convolutions.
, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely.

Experiments on two machine translation tasks show these models to be superior in quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train.

In this work we present the Transformer, a model architecture eschewing recurrence and instead relying entirely on an attention mechanism to draw global dependencies between input and output.

Can you explain the mathematical definition of Attention?

Scaled Dot-Product Attention

The Transformer uses a specific attention mechanism called "Scaled Dot-Product Attention". The input consists of queries and keys of dimension d_k, and values of dimension d_v.

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax( QKT √dk )V

We compute the dot products of the query with all keys, divide each by √d_k, and apply a softmax function to obtain the weights on the values.
