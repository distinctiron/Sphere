## Technique Selection

**Approach:** AI-Recommended Techniques
**Analysis Context:** AI-Powered Research Article Tracker & Reader with focus on tracking, ranking, summarizing, and interactive reading.

**Recommended Techniques:**

- **First Principles Thinking:** Strips away assumptions about current research consumption to rebuild the experience from fundamental truths. Expected outcome: A clear, unique value proposition.
- **SCAMPER Method:** Systematic approach to innovate on specific requirements (ranking, summarizing, etc.). Expected outcome: A robust list of differentiated features.
- **Provocation Technique:** Uses deliberate provocations to spark wild ideas for radical differentiation. Expected outcome: One or two "killer features".

**AI Rationale:** The sequence moves from foundational value definition (First Principles) to feature expansion (SCAMPER) and finally to radical differentiation (Provocation), ensuring a comprehensive and groundbreaking result.

## Technique Execution Results

**First Principles Thinking:**

- **Interactive Focus:** Stripping away "Article List" and "PDF Reading" to find fundamental truths of research consumption.
- **Key Breakthroughs:**
    - **The Filter (Triage):** The goal is "Efficient Insight Transfer". Replaced standard list with an **Impact Dashboard** featuring "Impact Cards" (Relevance Score + "Why it Matters").
    - **The Deep Dive (Learning):** The goal is "Active Comprehension". Replaced static PDF with an **Interactive Learning Layer** where highlighting triggers on-demand "micro-courses" or explainers tailored to user expertise.
- **User Creative Strengths:** Strong focus on the *outcome* of learning rather than the *process* of reading.
- **Energy Level:** High, focused on value and utility.

**SCAMPER Method:**

- **Substitute:** Replaced "Keywords" with **Novelty Index** (Groundbreaking potential) as the primary filter.
- **Combine:**
    - **Audio Triage:** Generate podcasts from articles for commute consumption (digestible first pass).
    - **Executable Papers:** Combine articles with code repositories.
- **Adapt:** **Research Intellisense** (borrowed from IDEs) - hover to get tailored definitions/explanations.
- **Modify:**
    - **Minify:** **Knowledge Diff** - Show only what is new compared to previous research.
    - **Magnify:** **Idea Genealogy** - Zoom out to see the family tree of the concept.
- **Eliminate:** **The Abstract** - Completely replaced by the "Impact Card" / Overview page.
- **Reverse:** **Reverse Search** - Papers compete for the user's attention based on interest/goals, rather than user searching for papers.
- **Put to other use:** Considered (investment, hiring) but decided to keep focus strictly on the research/learning use case.

**Provocation Technique:**

- **Interactive Focus:** Using deliberate provocations to spark "Killer Features".
- **Key Breakthroughs:**
    - **Provocation:** "The app should have NO TEXT."
    - **Result:** Rejected as a replacement. Core Principle established: **"Augment thinking, don't replace it."** Visualizations are valuable as an *additive* layer, but text is essential for foundational understanding.
    - **Provocation:** "The app should DISAGREE with the paper."
    - **Result:** **AI Skeptic / Counter-Point Finder** - Valuable as an exploratory feature to highlight biases or contradictions, but not a core default view.
